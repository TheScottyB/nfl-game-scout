# Last-30-Day Social Media & Locker-Room Sentiment Analysis

Use this guide when executing **Module 5**. The goal is to capture the
**emotional, psychological, and narrative momentum** surrounding both teams
over the **past 30 calendar days** — without confusing viral noise for real
signal.

---

## 30-Day Research Playbook

Anchor every query with the **current month and year** (and the 30-day window
preceding kickoff). Run searches across three complementary layers:

### Layer 1: Key Players' Public Social Activity & Media Tone
Check the star QBs, WR1/TE1/RB1, defensive captains, and newly traded/returning
players:
* Public X (`x.com`) previews and official team accounts (`@<Team>`).
* Press-conference and locker-room quotes reported by beat writers over the last
  30 days.
* Look for concrete signals:
  * **Positive:** Returning-from-injury excitement, trade-deadline chemistry
    hype (e.g., teammates hyping a newly acquired starter), players-only unity,
    playful banter / relaxed confidence.
  * **Negative:** Cryptic posts, deleted team bios/photos, frustration over
    targets/touches or defensive collapses, terse pressers, public contract or
    role tension.
* **Login-Wall Guardrail:** Never attempt to log in or bypass sign-in gates on
  X or Instagram. If Instagram profiles or full X timelines are gated behind a
  login wall, report cleanly what was publicly observable and lean on
  web-indexed beat coverage and official team feeds.

### Layer 2: Local Beat Reporters & Locker-Room Pulse
Search local beat coverage and team-specific outlets from the past 30 days:
* Is the coaching staff facing **hot-seat / play-calling criticism**?
* How is the locker room responding to recent wins/losses (e.g., riding a
  blowout win, stewing over a blown lead, surviving a brutal travel stretch)?
* Are there recurring **practice-report storylines** (e.g., altitude prep
  complaints, turf/injury frustration, backup stepping up)?

### Layer 3: Fanbase & Betting Market Sentiment
* **Fanbase Mood (30-Day Arc):** Classify each team's 30-day trajectory using
  one clear label:
  * `Surging` — riding consecutive convincing wins / high-profile boost
  * `Confident / Steady` — stable expectations, minimal drama
  * `Cautious / Skeptical` — winning ugly or worried about injuries/schedule
  * `Volatile / Divided` — big swings, QB/coordinator debates
  * `Panicking / Checked Out` — losing skid, firing rumors, apathy
* **Public vs. Sharp Money Split (if available):** Compare ticket % (public
  sentiment) vs. handle/money % (sharp sentiment) on spread and total to spot
  **overreaction traps**.

---

## Scoring Rubric (`-5` to `+5`)

Assign each team an explicit 30-day sentiment score:

* **`+4` to `+5`:** Euphoric / surging momentum, unified locker room, positive
  trade or injury-return spark. *(Watch for letdown/overpriced favorite risk!)*
* **`+1` to `+3`:** Healthy confidence, stable locker room, constructive tone.
* **`0`:** Neutral / mixed signals.
* **`-1` to `-3`:** Frustration creeping in, injury gloom, coordinator scrutiny.
* **`-4` to `-5`:** Crisis mode, public tension, hot-seat firestorm. *(Watch for
  "nobody believes in us" backs-against-the-wall bounce-back risk!)*
