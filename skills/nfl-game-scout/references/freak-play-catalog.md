# Crazy Surprise Story & Freak-Play Catalog

Module 7 is the signature highlight of every **NFL Game Scout** dossier. The
goal is **never** a random gimmick ("maybe there's a hail mary") — it is a
**lightning-in-a-bottle rare event** built strictly out of **3+ real, verified
ingredients** from tonight's exact matchup.

> **Benchmark Example (SNF Mexico City, Nov 22, 2026):**
> High altitude (~7,350 ft) + big-leg Vikings kicker (Will Reichard, 62-yd
> career long → ~70-yd altitude ceiling) + end-of-half fair-catch free-kick
> rule → *predicted a ~70-yd record threat / fair-catch free kick* → Reichard
> literally attempted a **71-yard fair-catch free kick** at `0:03` of Q2 that
> landed **1 yard short of the crossbar, dead center**.

---

## Ingredient Checklist (Combine at Least 3)

1. **Venue / Physics Quirks:** Extreme altitude (Mexico City ~7,350 ft, Denver
   ~5,280 ft), swirling stadium winds, rain/snow slick turf, sun glare, or
   indoor fast track.
2. **Specialist Profiles & Milestones:** Big-leg kicker near NFL record range
   (68 yds), struggling kicker on thin ice, booming punter pinning inside the 5,
   retiring veteran punter/kicker in a farewell primetime home game, or a
   punter/backup QB with college passing/gadget history.
3. **Coach Aggression & Desperation:** Heavy 4th-down/2-pt coaches, big
   underdogs (5.5+ pts) who cannot trade punts with an elite QB, or coaches
   with documented fake-punt/trick-play tendencies.
4. **Referee Blind Spots:** A whistle-swallowing crew (like Bill Vinovich on
   holding/contact) that gives trick-play blocking extra leeway, OR a
   flag-happy crew (like Alex Moore on pre-snap/RTP/holding) that sets up
   bizarre field-position swings, untimed downs, or penalty-assisted scoring.
5. **Matchup History & Roster Oddities:** Previous bizarre games between these
   franchises (e.g., zero-punt shootout), newly traded QB facing a team he just
   played weeks ago, decimated position room forcing emergency personnel
   (backup OL/TE/DB or emergency 3rd QB).

## Rare Rulebook & Freak-Play Menu

Draw from real NFL mechanics when the ingredients genuinely line up:

* **Fair-Catch Free Kick (65–72+ yds):** Live when altitude/tailwind + big-leg
  kicker + end-of-half punt from deep in opponent territory align.
* **Fake Punt Pass / Fake FG Throw:** Live when an aggressive underdog coach
  cannot afford to punt to an elite offense, especially with an athletic or
  farewell-tour punter/holder.
* **Defensive 2-Point Conversion Return ("Pick-Two" / Scoop-Two):** Live in
  high-total shootouts (O/U 50+) with aggressive 2-point coaches and loose DB
  officiating — flips a 1-point margin into a 3-point swing.
* **Record Field Goal Attempt (68+ yds) or Untimed-Down Walk-Off:** Live with a
  flag-happy RTP/DPI ref crew (extending a 0:00 clock on a defensive penalty)
  or thin air/tailwinds.
* **Zero-Punt or Zero-Turnover Shootout / Scorigami:** Live when two top-5
  passing offenses meet bottom-tier defenses in clean weather with low-flag
  refs.
* **Safety / 1-Point Safety / Blocked-Punt-for-TD:** Live when a team missing
  starting offensive linemen faces an elite pass rush/special teams unit in
  wind/rain or loud road environments.
* **Gadget / Non-QB Touchdown Pass (WR/RB/TE/Backup QB Package):** Live when a
  team's WR1 is out and the OC has to manufacture red-zone explosives.
* **Dynamic Kickoff Chaos / Surprise Onside Kick (4th Quarter):** Live under
  current NFL kickoff rules when a trailing team burns timeouts or faces a
  shaky return/coverage unit.

## Output Format for Module 7

* **Ingredients on the Board:** 3 concise bullets citing verified facts from
  Modules 1–6.
* **Headline Crazy Surprise Call:** Exact play/event, who executes it, when it
  triggers, why tonight's physics/refs/coaches unlock it, and an honest
  probability estimate (e.g., `~5–8%`).
* **Bonus Freak-Play Sleeper:** 1–2 sentences on a secondary wild-rule or
  statistical outlier to watch for.
