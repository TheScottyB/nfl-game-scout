# Full Org-Chart Matchup Guide (GM → Assistant Position Coaches)

When running **Module 6**, verify **current-season** coaching directories for
both teams via official team sites (`<team>.com/team/coaches-roster`),
Pro-Football-Reference, or Wikipedia's current-season team article. Coaching
staffs turn over heavily every offseason — never assume last year's assistants
are still in the building.

---

## The 4 Organizational Tiers

### Tier 1 — Front Office / General Manager
Evaluate how roster architecture directly affects *tonight's* game:
* **GM Name & Tenure:** Compare each GM's roster-building philosophy
  (draft-and-develop vs. win-now trades vs. cap discipline).
* **Recent Transactions (Last 30–60 Days):** Trade-deadline acquisitions,
  practice-squad elevations, waiver pickups, IR activations.
* **Depth Stress Test:** Which GM's bench holds up better against tonight's
  actual injury/inactive list (e.g., backup OT vs. opposing EDGE, CB4/S3 vs.
  opposing WR3)?

### Tier 2 — Head Coach & Coordinators (HC / OC / DC / STC)
Map the marquee schematic battles:
* **Head Coach:** Overall record, primetime/road record, 4th-down & 2-point
  aggression, timeout/clock management, challenge success.
* **Offensive Coordinator vs. Opposing Defensive Coordinator:**
  * Does the HC call plays or does the OC/DC?
  * Scheme clash: motion/play-action/RPO/quick-game vs. blitz rate, simulated
    pressures, two-high shell vs. single-high man.
  * Historical head-to-head record between these play-callers.
* **Special Teams Coordinator (STC):** Return aggression, fake-punt / fake-FG
  history, coverage-unit discipline (crucial when paired with tonight's ref
  crew and stadium weather).

### Tier 3 — Position Coaches (Unit-vs-Unit Matrix)
Always map **opposing position rooms directly against each other**:

| Battleground | Team A Room Coach | Team B Opposing Room Coach | What to Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pass Attack vs. Coverage** | QB Coach + WR/TE Coaches | DB / Secondary Coach + Def. Pass-Game Coord. | Route-tree tweaks, backup DB readiness, red-zone separation |
| **Pass Protection vs. Pass Rush** | Offensive Line Coach | Defensive Line Coach / Pass-Rush Specialist | Stunts, blitz pickup, backup tackle help/chip plans |
| **Run Game vs. Front Seven** | RB Coach / Run-Game Coord. | Linebackers Coach / Def. Run-Game Coord. | Gap vs. zone fits, RB pass-pro, QB spy discipline |
| **Specialists** | Special Teams / Kicking Coach | Opposing STC | Snap-hold-kick operation, directional punting |

### Tier 4 — Assistant Position Coaches, Specialists & Quality Control (QC)
This is the hidden edge most game previews miss. Verify current names from team
coach rosters and check:
* **Assistant OL / Assistant DL / Assistant DB / Assistant WR / Assistant LB /
  Assistant QB / Assistant STC** coaches.
* **Offensive & Defensive Quality Control (QC) Coaches & Game Management
  Specialists:** Self-scouting, advance opponent tendency breakdown, situational
  4th-down/clock charts.
* **Cross-Staff Coaching-Tree & Former-Coworker Intelligence ("Tells"):**
  * Flag any coach — from GM down to QC assistant — who **previously worked
    for the opposing team** or trained under the **same coaching tree**
    (Shanahan/McVay, Reid, Belichick, Flores, Quinn, Harbaugh, etc.).
  * Note what concrete knowledge transfers: snap-count cadences, blitz
    indicators, protection calls, player tendencies (e.g., an assistant who
    coached the opposing QB/WR/OL last season).
